# Data request email, NSW Health Water Unit

To: HSSG-WaterQual@health.nsw.gov.au
Subject: Request for a one-off extract of total hardness data, NSW Drinking Water Database

---

Dear Water Unit,

I'm building a consumer information tool that tells someone the typical
hardness of their tap water from their postcode, and I'm writing to ask whether
a one-off extract of hardness data from the NSW Drinking Water Database is
something you're able to provide.

What I'm after is narrow:

- **Parameter:** total hardness as CaCO₃, in mg/L. Nothing else — no
  microbiological, health-related or fluoride results.
- **Grouping:** by water utility and supply system, rather than by individual
  sample.
- **Period:** the most recent complete reporting year. An average per supply
  system is fine; I don't need the underlying sample data.
- **Frequency:** once. This isn't a request for an ongoing feed or API access.

For context, the tool is being built for a skincare brand, since water hardness
affects how skin and hair feel after washing. It's a free public lookup rather
than anything gated, but I'd rather be upfront that there's a commercial party
behind it.

On how the figures would be presented:

- Attributed to the source, naming NSW Health and the relevant water utility.
- Shown with a disclaimer stating the figure is a published figure for a supply
  zone, not a measurement of the user's own tap, that hardness varies within a
  zone and over time, and that their water utility is who to contact for a
  definitive figure.
- Used only to describe hardness. No health claims of any kind are made or
  implied anywhere in the tool.

I appreciate the database is an operational system rather than an open data
product, and that the underlying results belong to the individual water
utilities. I'm also conscious this may not be the right inbox for a request of
this kind — if there's someone better placed to answer it, I'd be grateful if
you could pass this on, or point me their way.

If an extract isn't something that can be released at all, a steer on the right
route would be just as useful — whether that's approaching the local water
utilities directly, a formal information access request, or simply working from
the annual reports councils publish.

Happy to take whatever format is easiest at your end, including a plain CSV or
spreadsheet export.

Many thanks for your time.

Kind regards,

Nick Krull
KDNA
hello@krulldna.com

## Before sending

The address is published on the NSW drinking water database page and checks out.
It is, however, the technical support inbox for the database itself — password
resets and system help for the utilities and laboratories that enter results —
rather than a public data request desk.

Worth phoning the Water Unit first on **02 9391 9939** to ask who handles this.
An email addressed to a named person is treated very differently from one that
arrives cold in a support queue.
